# DasTech — Digital Products & Software Engineering Studio

Building high-performance digital products, SaaS starters, and enterprise software solutions that move businesses forward.

## Overview
DasTech is a modern software development agency and digital product marketplace offering production-ready templates, SaaS blueprints, DevOps toolkits, and custom cloud engineering services.

## Project Structure
- `index.html` - Homepage with Hero, Trust Stats, Featured Products, Projects, Services, Why DasTech, Client Portfolio, and Lead Capture.
- `products.html` - Digital Products Marketplace with live search, category pills, and sorting.
- `product-details.html` - Comprehensive product information, feature matrices, requirements, and checkout triggers.
- `projects.html` - Client case studies and portfolio showcases.
- `project-details.html` - In-depth engineering case studies.
- `cart.html` & `checkout.html` - Full-featured cart, coupon engine, and secure digital checkout flow.
- `services-page.html` & `about.html` - Dedicated public services and company overview pages.
- `dashboard.html` - Modern executive dashboard and administration portal.
- `assets/` - Design system, styles (`style.css`), data models (`dastech-data.js`), and commerce state (`cart.js`).
